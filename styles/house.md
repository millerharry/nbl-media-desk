# House style (neutral)

- Australian English spelling; "points", "boards", "dimes" are fine in social posts, avoid slang in recaps.
- Lead with the result and the defining moment, then the stars, then the context.
- Numbers: digits for stats ("28 points"), scores as "94-88".
- Never speculate about injuries, contracts, off-court life or betting.
- Social posts: under 280 characters, at most two hashtags, no emoji in recaps.
